# BASF Financial Performance & Risk Analysis

## Overview

This project presents a five-year financial performance and risk analysis of **BASF SE**, covering FY2021–FY2025.

The analysis evaluates BASF's financial position using financial statement data, financial ratios, working capital metrics, cash flow analysis, and leverage indicators.

## Project Objectives

- Assess BASF's financial health over FY2021–FY2025
- Analyze short-term liquidity and working capital
- Evaluate profitability and returns
- Examine operational efficiency
- Analyze operating, investing, and financing cash flows
- Assess leverage and solvency
- Identify key areas of financial strength and risk

## Key Findings

The analysis indicates that BASF maintained positive working capital throughout FY2021–FY2025, with working capital reaching €15.273 billion in FY2025.

Liquidity remained adequate, with the FY2025 Current Ratio at 1.93x and Quick Ratio at 1.19x.

Profitability recovered from the weakness observed in FY2022. FY2025 Net Profit Margin reached 2.71%, while ROA and ROE also improved.

Operational efficiency remained an area requiring attention. Total Asset Turnover declined over the analysis period, while FY2025 showed some improvement in receivables and inventory efficiency.

Operating cash flow declined from €8.111 billion in FY2023 to €5.610 billion in FY2025, highlighting weaker operating cash generation.

Leverage increased over the period, with Debt-to-EBITDA reaching 3.58x in FY2025.
